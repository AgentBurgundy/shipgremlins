# ShipGremlins v0.28.4

Explain why approved work is waiting and keep PM investigations moving beyond
known test-environment limits.

- Project autonomy and coding controls identify approved tickets held after a
  failed or canceled attempt, with a direct link to review the previous run.
  Observations come from existing queue checks, expire quickly, and clear when
  a newer explicit attempt appears. Failed reads remain unknown. Approval,
  delivery gates and duplicate-run protection are unchanged.
- PMs receive those observed holds and the controller's confirmed epic context.
  Patrol guidance distinguishes unsupported fixture checks from app defects,
  keeps blocked criteria unverified, and moves to another reachable investigation
  instead of repeating an unchanged blocker. Reports distinguish saved evidence
  from tickets, coding runs and product changes.
- The disposable dashboard fixture supports final existing-repository creation
  and revision-guarded setup review using explicitly simulated source and AI
  adapters. Real providers, worker execution, ticket delivery and releases remain
  disabled. Empty recovery queues load without contacting providers.
- PM brief feedback stays beside the fixed actions on phone and desktop. Reload
  and deletion refusals, validation errors and failed saves remain visible while
  unsaved edits are preserved.

Install through **Settings → Updates** and restart the controller. New PM runs
receive the updated guidance. Existing failed or unconfirmed attempts are not
automatically repeated: review their output and any external work before choosing
an explicit retry. Rebuild the disposable dashboard fixture to test its expanded
onboarding journey.
